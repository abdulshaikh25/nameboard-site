Will a website actually help me get hired?

It won't replace a good resume, but it helps you stand out where it counts: startups, product companies, design and developer roles, and applications abroad. It also gives you something concrete to walk through in interviews.

Can you make my resume ATS-friendly?

Yes. We format a clean PDF that applicant tracking systems can read, and keep it in sync with your site.

What if my projects aren't live anymore?

Screenshots, a short screen recording or the GitHub repo work fine. We'll make the most of what you have.

Will my phone number be public?

Only if you want it to be. We can use a contact form instead, so recruiters reach you without seeing your number.

What happens after I get a job?

Keep it as your professional home, or let it lapse. Your domain stays registered in your name either way.
