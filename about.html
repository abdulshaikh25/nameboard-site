About Nameboard

Cheap websites come with catches. We started Nameboard to remove them.

Every street in India has shops with beautiful name boards and no website. We make that next step simple, fair and fast.

Why we exist

A website shouldn't be a gamble.

Students keep their best work in Google Drive folders. Freelancers send screenshots of their rates. Shops that have served a neighbourhood for decades are invisible to anyone searching on a phone.

The websites on offer were either expensive and slow, or cheap in ways that only showed up later: renewals that doubled, domains registered to the agency instead of the owner, and support that vanished the moment the payment cleared.

Nameboard is our answer. You see your website before you pay. Your domain is in your name. Next year's price is printed next to this year's. And a real person answers on WhatsApp.

How we work

Four promises we don't bend.

See it before you pay

No advance, ever, on our standard plans. If you don't like the preview, you walk away.

Your domain, your name

Registered to you and billed at cost. If you leave, it goes with you.

Next year's price, printed

Every plan lists its renewal on the pricing page and on your invoice.

Designed like a product

Every site is checked for speed, readability, accessibility and a clear next step before it goes live.

Who's behind it

Built by a product designer, not a template factory.

Nameboard was started in Mumbai by a product manager with more than six years in UX and product design. Every site goes through the same thinking we'd give a real product: who is visiting, what they need to see first, and what we want them to do next.

We use modern tools, including AI, to move fast on the parts that don't need a human, so we can spend our time on the parts that do: your words, your photos and getting the details right.

Where we are. Mumbai, working with clients across India on WhatsApp, Monday to Saturday, 10 am to 8 pm.
