Is the preview really free?

Yes. We design your homepage and send it to you without asking for any payment. You only pay if you approve it. If you don't, you owe us nothing.

What do I get for ₹999?

A one-page website for your shop, portfolio or career: your photos, work or prices, buttons to message or call you, hosting and a security certificate for a year, and one round of changes. Your domain is extra, at cost.

What happens after the first year?

Starter and the personal plans renew at ₹999 a year. Pro, Business and Signature renew at ₹1,999, which covers hosting, your domain and small fixes. We remind you a month before, and if you leave, your domain stays yours.

I'm a student. What goes on my portfolio?

Your best three to six projects, a short introduction, your skills and a way to contact you, plus your resume as a download and links to GitHub, Behance or LinkedIn. See student portfolios.

Do I need photos or a logo?

No. Clear phone photos of your shop, products or work are perfect. If you have none yet, we start with tasteful placeholders and swap them in when you're ready. We can also design a logo for ₹999.

What if I'm not happy?

Before you pay, you can walk away at no cost. After you pay, if your website isn't live on time, you get a full refund. Domain fees go to the registrar and can't be refunded. Read the refund policy.
