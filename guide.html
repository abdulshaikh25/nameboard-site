After you go live

Your website is live. Here's how to get it seen.

Eight small things to do in your first week. Most take less than five minutes, and together they make the biggest difference.

Eight steps for your first week

STEP 01

Add it to Google

Open your Google Business Profile, tap Edit profile and paste your website address. This is the single biggest step for being found nearby.

STEP 02

Put it in your Instagram bio

Edit profile, then Links. Replace any “DM for details” with your website, so people see prices before they message.

STEP 03

Add it to WhatsApp Business

Settings, then Business tools, then Profile. Add your website so it shows every time someone opens your chat.

STEP 04

Print your QR poster

We send a print-ready QR poster with every site. Put it on your counter, your door or your visiting card.

STEP 05

Use it on your resume and LinkedIn

For students and freshers: add it under your name on your resume, in your LinkedIn Contact info and in your email signature.

STEP 06

Ask five customers for a review

Send your Google review link to five happy customers this week. Reviews show on your site and help you rank.

STEP 07

Share it on your status

Post your new website on your WhatsApp status with a small offer. Your contacts are your first visitors.

STEP 08

Tell us what to update

New prices, timings or projects? Message us. Small fixes are free for your first 30 days.
