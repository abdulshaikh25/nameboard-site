Questions

Pricing questions.

Ask us anything on WhatsApp. A real person replies within 2 hours.

See all questions
Why is the preview free?

Because it's the fastest way to earn your trust. Most people who see a preview of their own site want it, and the ones who don't owe us nothing.

What does the renewal pay for?

Hosting, your security certificate, backups and keeping the site running. On Pro and above it also covers your domain renewal.

Can I upgrade later?

Yes. Move from Starter to Pro or Business anytime, and we credit what you've already paid for the current year.

Do you offer monthly payments?

Not yet. Plans are paid yearly, which keeps the price low. The care plan is the only monthly item.

Are there discounts for students?

Yes. Student portfolios are ₹799 with a campus ambassador code or in groups of five from one college.
