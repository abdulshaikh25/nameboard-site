General

What is Nameboard?

We design and launch websites for shops, students, freshers and creators across India. You see your website free before you pay, and plans start at ₹999 a year.

Is the preview really free?

Yes. We design your homepage and send it to you without asking for any payment. You only pay if you approve it.

How fast is it?

Your preview arrives within 24 hours of sending your details. Starter and the personal plans go live within 48 hours of approval; Pro in 3 to 4 days; Business in 7 days.

Do you use templates?

We start from proven layouts and tailor every site to your photos, work and words. Business and Signature sites are designed section by section.

Pricing and payment

What do I get for ₹999?

A one-page website with your photos, work or prices, buttons to message or call you, hosting and a security certificate for a year, and one round of changes. Your domain is extra, at cost.

What happens after the first year?

Starter and personal plans renew at ₹999 a year; Pro, Business and Signature at ₹1,999. We remind you a month before.

How do I pay?

By UPI or a secure payment link, after you approve your preview. You get an invoice for every payment.

Are there hidden charges?

No. Everything that could cost extra is listed on the pricing page under “What isn't included”.

Domains and hosting

What is a domain?

Your website's address, like sharmatailors.in. We register it in your name so it always belongs to you.

Why is the domain extra on Starter?

So you pay exactly what the registrar charges, usually ₹600 to ₹900 a year, with no markup. Pro and above include it for the first year.

I already have a domain. Can I use it?

Yes. We connect it for free, and you keep it registered wherever it is now.

Can I have an email like hello@myshop.in?

Yes. We set it up with a provider such as Google Workspace or Zoho, and you pay the provider directly.

Students and freshers

What goes on a student portfolio?

Three to six projects, a short introduction, your skills and a way to contact you, plus your resume and links to GitHub, Behance or LinkedIn.

Will a website help me get hired?

It won't replace a good resume, but it helps you stand out with startups, product companies, design and developer roles, and applications abroad.

Is there a student discount?

Student portfolios are ₹799 with a campus ambassador code or in groups of five from one college.

Freelancers and creators

Why do I need a website if I'm on Instagram?

Instagram is where people discover you; a website is where they decide. It shows prices, packages and reviews in one place and appears on Google.

Can clients pay a booking advance?

Yes. We can add a UPI QR code or a payment link.

Changes and support

Can I make changes later?

Yes. Small fixes are free for 30 days after launch. After that, the care plan covers monthly updates for ₹299, or we quote per change.

Who do I contact for help?

Message us on WhatsApp. A real person replies within 2 hours, Monday to Saturday, 10 am to 8 pm.

Can my website be in Hindi or Marathi?

Yes. A second language version is ₹999.

What if I'm not happy?

Before you pay, walk away at no cost. After you pay, if your site isn't live on time, you get a full refund. Refund policy.
