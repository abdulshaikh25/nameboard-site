Who we are

Nameboard (“we”, “us”) designs, builds and hosts websites. We're based in Mumbai, India. You can reach us on WhatsApp or at hello@nameboard.co.in.

These terms apply when you request a preview, buy a plan or use any of our services. By paying for a plan, you agree to them.

Free previews

Previews are free and carry no obligation. Until you pay, the design belongs to us; you may not copy or publish it. We may reuse general layouts, but never your name, photos or personal details.

Plans and payment

Prices are listed on our pricing page in Indian rupees, before tax. For standard and personal plans, you pay after approving your preview. Signature is paid 50% at the start and 50% on design approval.

Delivery

Delivery times start when you approve your preview and we have the content we need. If we're waiting on photos, text or approvals from you, the clock pauses until we receive them.

Your content

You confirm that you have the right to use everything you send us, including photos, logos and text. You keep ownership of your content. You give us permission to use it to build and run your website.

Domains

We register domains in your name. The registrar's own terms also apply. Domain fees are paid to the registrar and can't be refunded once registered.

Hosting and renewals

Each plan covers 12 months of hosting. Renewal is at the price listed when you bought the plan. If you don't renew, your site stays up for 15 days, then goes offline. We'll send your site files on request for 30 days after that.

Changes and support

Each plan includes the rounds of changes listed for it. Small fixes are free for 30 days after launch. Later changes are covered by the care plan or quoted in advance.

Acceptable use

Limits of liability

We work hard to keep your site running, but we can't guarantee it will never go down. Our total liability for any claim is limited to what you paid us in the 12 months before the claim.
