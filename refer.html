Campus ambassadors

Represent Nameboard at your college.

Help classmates get their portfolios online before placement season, and earn while you do it.

WHAT YOU DO

Spread the word

Share Nameboard in class groups and at fests, collect portfolio requests and pass them to us on WhatsApp. About two hours a week.

WHAT YOU GET

₹150 per portfolio

Paid for every portfolio that goes live, plus your own portfolio free, a ₹799 code for classmates, and a certificate and LinkedIn recommendation after a semester.

The fine print

Simple rules.

  • Referral rewards are paid once the referred site is live and paid for.
  • Your friend can't combine the ₹100 referral discount with a campus code.
  • You can't refer yourself or your own business.
  • We may change or end the programme with 30 days' notice. Rewards already earned are always paid.
