I'm already on Google Maps. Do I need a website?

Your Maps listing gets you found; a website gets you chosen. It shows your full prices, photos and offers, and makes you look established next to listings with no website.

Can you set up my Google Business Profile?

Yes. It's included in Pro and above, or ₹499 as an add-on to Starter.

Can customers order on the website?

Orders come to you on WhatsApp with the item already typed. For full online payments and stock, ask us about the Business plan.

Can my website be in Hindi or Marathi?

Yes. A second language version is ₹999, and we can write it for you or use your text.

Do I need a logo?

No. We can set your name in a clean type style, or design a logo for ₹999.
